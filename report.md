Self-learning is multifaceted and crucial for staying relevant in this rapidly evolving field. ## 1.2. Effective self-learning and its challenges:

### 1.2.1. What is the most effective way you currently self-learn?

<!-- *Write ~50 words describing what works best for you when self-learning.*  -->
I learn best by following step-by-step tutorials and then applying what I've learned to build something practical. I prefer to start with basic concepts and gradually add more complex features. I also find it helpful to use generative AI to give me a handy starting point of the concepts.

### 1.2.2. What is the most difficult challenge for you when you are self-learning?

<!-- *Write ~50 words describing what you find difficult when self-learning.* -->
The most challenging part of self-learning is debugging and solving unexpected problems. When I encounter errors or issues that aren't covered in tutorials, I need to research solutions and sometimes try multiple approaches before finding the right one. This can be frustrating but also helps me learn more deeply.
